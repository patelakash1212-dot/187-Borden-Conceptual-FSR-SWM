# Portfolio Disclaimer

This repository contains a **self-directed conceptual engineering portfolio project** prepared to demonstrate civil, municipal, land-development, stormwater-management, and Autodesk Civil 3D workflows.

The material is **not**:

- an issued-for-construction design;
- a municipal submission;
- a tender or contract document;
- a sealed professional engineering design;
- a substitute for field survey, utility verification, geotechnical investigation, or detailed hydraulic analysis; or
- intended to be relied upon for construction, permitting, approval, or property-development decisions.

Design criteria, dimensions, elevations, service information, and calculation assumptions used in the project require independent verification before any real-world engineering use.

The project demonstrates a conceptual workflow and engineering reasoning process only.
