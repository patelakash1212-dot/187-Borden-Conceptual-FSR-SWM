# Calculation Summary

## 1. Pre- and Post-Development Runoff

The conceptual drainage area is approximately **0.115 ha**.

| Condition | Weighted Runoff Coefficient |
|---|---:|
| Pre-development | 0.46 |
| Post-development | 0.78 |

Peak runoff was evaluated using:

`Q = 2.78 C I A`

with a conceptual time of concentration of **10 minutes**.

| Return Period | Pre-Development | Post-Development |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5-year | 16.28 L/s | 27.43 L/s |
| 100-year | 29.17 L/s | 49.14 L/s |

The conceptual quantity-control target is therefore:

**Allowable release = pre-development 5-year flow = 16.28 L/s**

## 2. Trial Outlet-Control Calculation

The project applies the orifice equation:

`Q = Cd A sqrt(2 g h)`

The report uses approximately:

- `Cd = 0.80`
- high-water level = 335.71 m
- trial orifice invert = 333.32 m
- effective head used in the detailed calculation = approximately 2.35 m
- theoretical trial diameter = 60 mm
- resulting discharge = approximately 15.35 L/s

### Important Design Status

The 60 mm opening is a theoretical hydraulic trial. The report also notes a 75 mm minimum opening criterion. Under the assumed head, a 75 mm opening would discharge approximately 23.96 L/s and would exceed the 16.28 L/s allowable release. The outlet-control configuration therefore requires further detailed-design iteration.

## 3. Modified Rational Method Storage

The storage analysis evaluates multiple 100-year storm durations. The report identifies the critical preliminary storage requirement at approximately **25 minutes**, with a maximum required storage of approximately **25.16 m3** for the 60 mm theoretical trial outlet.
